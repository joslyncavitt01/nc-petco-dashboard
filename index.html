Tracks the March 2026 PEDIGREE Foundation D2A grant goals: 180+ pets transported from Texas source shelters to NC and placed in adoptive homes in 2026, 100+ local NC shelter transfers-in, and growing the active NC foster corps from 5 to 15+ caregivers. The 180 goal counts only TX-origin pets that have actually been adopted, not just arrived — arrivals still waiting on an adoption are shown separately. "Active" foster caregiver = fostered 2+ times in 2026, not just anyone currently holding an animal. Texas vs. NC origin is determined from each animal's prior-shelter record (backfilled by transfer partner where that record is missing); a small, mostly-2025 share of historical transfers can't be classified either way and are excluded from both totals below.
