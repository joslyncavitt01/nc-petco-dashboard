How these numbers are counted
  • Grant period: January 1 to December 31, 2026, matching the proposal's "Goals for 2026" and the award letter's calendar-year terms.
  • Directly into homes: a Texas pet's first move after arriving in NC is to a foster or an adopter. "Within 1 day" means the arrival day or the next day; "within 3 days" allows a short stay at the center. Pets that arrived too recently to judge are left out until their window closes.
  • Overall transport program: pets in Austin's ShelterLuv that passed through the Elgin Transport Hub and were then transferred to another shelter during 2026.
  • Texas → NC placed in homes: Texas pets that arrived in NC in 2026 and have been adopted. Texas vs. NC origin comes from each pet's prior-shelter record.
  • Active foster caregivers: people who fostered 2 or more times in 2026.
  • On pace: the 2026 total so far, projected to December 31 at the current daily rate.
Every animal that's passed through the NC center, all-time. For animals transferred in through Austin Pets Alive's own ShelterLuv system, this combines their full Austin history (photo, memos, intake/outcome timeline) with their NC record.
